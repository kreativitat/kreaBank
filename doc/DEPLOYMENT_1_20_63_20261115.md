# KreaBank 1.20.63 — recorded customer transfers

Installed on `fin.degema.pt` on 2026-11-15 from
[`4a074f3`](https://github.com/kreativitat/kreaBank/commit/4a074f3a31528cd0223d077555c5eb30b3a4e5ba).
Baseline: 1.20.62; PHP 8.1.34 / Dolibarr 24.0.2.

## Result

Signed manual review now enriches existing customer bank-transfer receipts with
their native payment reference/number, actual owned customer and complete invoice
allocations. A receipt allocated across several invoices remains one existing
native movement. Its compact card links to the native customer-payment card and
shows the invoice count. Native transfer codes `TRF` and `VIR` are supported;
other payment methods retain their existing behavior.

This records no payment or invoice allocation. Exact positive decimal amounts,
company currency, account ownership, customer visibility and complete allocation
facts are required. Conflicting, foreign, orphan, duplicate, credit-note or
incoherent evidence supplies no enrichment. The displayed receipt facts are signed,
checked for current visibility before cached display, and rechecked under the
existing execution locks. Wrong-bank evidence remains a separate blocking review.
Unattended and automatic-safe discovery keep their original evidence and scores.
