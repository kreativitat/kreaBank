# KreaBank 1.20.59 — wrong-bank payment review

Source: [`a1957aa`](https://github.com/kreativitat/kreaBank/commit/a1957aa86d238482f37c93b964267619dbbe50a9).
Target: `fin.degema.pt`; baseline 1.20.58; PHP 8.1.34 / Dolibarr 24.0.2.

## Result

Wrong-bank invoice, salary and tax payments use the normal compact candidate
card, with their recorded bank reference and an accessible exclamation mark.
Eligible references open the existing signed account-correction dialog.
Reconciliation remains disabled until fresh same-account evidence clears the
warning. Previously signed selections, retained drafts and legacy manual cards
cannot override it. Competing or truncated results never choose a payment.

Salary detection preserves native read rights, employee hierarchy and direct
company ownership. Correction binds and locks native payment, parent, employee
and bank-link facts; it changes only the eligible movement's account through
the existing adapter. Reconciled, posted, deposited, claimed, imported,
restricted, foreign or malformed movements retain their protections. Tax
warnings require specific payment/liability identity rather than a generic
authority or tax label with the same amount and date.
