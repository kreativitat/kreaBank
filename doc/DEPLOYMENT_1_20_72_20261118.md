## Result

The existing company jobs now handle evidenced terminal/UNICRE credits and proven
transfers between selected accounts owned by the same company. Native receipts
and reciprocal transfers are reused when eligible; complete new transfers create
two `VIR` legs atomically. Restricted-account mappings retain their existing policy.

Saved automation is enabled with the explicit own-transfer option and active `MB`
receipt mode. Existing job identities and their 15-minute frequency are retained;
no scheduler was added. A full read-only assessment after the live runs found no
remaining eligible receipt or owned-transfer operation.

Live verification checked exact source/native amounts, account ownership,
currency, complete allocations, unique claims, audits and reciprocal transfer
links. New bank records were limited to positive `MB` receipts and complete `VIR`
transfers. Invoice, invoice-payment, salary-payment and tax-payment counts did not
change during the operational runs. Existing expense quick entries, conflicting
payments/adjustments and incomplete ownership/counterpart evidence remain for
review; automation does not repair those records by creating another debit.
