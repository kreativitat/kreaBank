## Result

Reconciliation, statement and History status tabs now display scoped counts.
Empty exceptional tabs are hidden unless selected; their original routes and
classifications remain available. Source status and private draft counts do not
invoke matching. Derived filters retain unknown counts until complete evidence
is available. Pending includes private drafts; Review means link-integrity
failure, not an unmatched payment. Raw Pending/Reconciled source admission and
count classification now agree before scanning or pagination.

Reconciled list rows offer a native-style reviewed Undo dialog through the
existing History snapshots, scoped single-use nonces and locked service. Groups
retain complete-batch Undo; payment differences retain their existing native
reversal. Cancel, X and Escape are local closes. The amount/match filters align
with their headers. History groups each payment/document and its bank entry by
explicit native movement identity, preserving every associated link.

Customer-transfer metadata accepts customers explicitly shared through native
`getEntity('societe')`. Payments, invoices and bank accounts still require direct
current-company ownership. Sharing and customer visibility are refreshed at
cached display and locked execution; no sharing configuration was changed.

replacing native reference labels with invoice references lowered an otherwise

approved live reconciliation; neither scoring thresholds nor ambiguity guards
