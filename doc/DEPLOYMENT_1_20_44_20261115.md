## Rollback

Retain the additive table and audit history when restoring source. Disable new
approvals first if either feature is later configured. If payment-difference
reconciliations exist, retain their History/native reversal implementation until
they are handled; older ordinary undo cannot reverse their payment and invoice
closing effects. See [bank rules](BANK_RULES.md) and
[payment differences](WRITE_OFF.md).
